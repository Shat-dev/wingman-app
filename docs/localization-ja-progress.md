Agreeing first to keep the conversation smooth is the polite norm in Japan, and open disagreement with someone you have just met is rarer; the lesson's actual advice (hold your view, stay curious, don't try to win) travels well, so this is a light flag.

A deliberate tactic to make her want more; with lesson 21_1 it is the content most likely to be read as 恋愛工学 (the Japanese name for pickup theory, widely criticised there). Translated as written.

These are the stock openers of pickup guides in Japan too. The lessons present them as ways to lower pressure, and 11_4 says not to make it sound like a trick; whether a made-up debate or a made-up time limit sits well with the app's honesty elsewhere (lesson 6_3, "Curiosity is not a tactic") is a question for the English as well.
