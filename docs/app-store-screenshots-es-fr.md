# App Store screenshots and header: Spanish + French text

Written 2026-11-15 for Arthur's Figma pass. It covers the header plus the nine screenshot frames in the current English set. Text inside the phones is copied from the app's own Spanish and French strings (`Localizable.xcstrings`, `Content.xcstrings`, `localization/{es,fr}/db/`), so each screenshot matches what users will see. The captions are new marketing text and still need a native speaker to check them.

Words used throughout:
- **Confidence:** *seguridad* in Spanish and *confianza* in French, as in the app.
- **Address:** *tú* in Spanish and *tu* in French, as in the app.
- **Brand:** "Wingman" stays as it is, with a capital W.

## 3. Natural

| English | Spanish | French |
|---|---|---|
| **Natural** (big serif) | **Presencia** | **Présence** |
| presence over time | natural, con el tiempo | naturelle, avec le temps |
| Stop waiting, Start Winning! | Deja de esperar, pasa a la acción. | Arrête d’attendre, passe à l’action ! |

- **Big word:** Spanish and French put the adjective after the noun, so the noun becomes the big serif word.
- **Last line:** "Start Winning" read literally (*empieza a ganar* / *commence à gagner*) sounds like a game or a bet. "Take action" is the natural way to say it. Use the literal version if you want it to match the English.
- **French spacing:** French needs a space before "!".
