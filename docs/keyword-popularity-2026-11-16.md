## Keyword fields now in App Store Connect (version 1.16)

App Store search combines words across the name, subtitle and keyword field, so single words like *conocer* + *gente* also cover *conocer gente*. No word repeats one already in the name or subtitle.

Names are unchanged. *Hablar con mujeres* (MX 21), *ligar* (MX 38 / ES 52), *seguridad* (MX 38), *femme* (FR 33) and *confiance en soi* (FR 25) all measure. The Spain subtitle was changed on 2026-11-16 from *Habla con mujeres sin miedo* (*habla* isn't a measured form) to **Hablar con chicas sin miedo** (*hablar* 33, *chicas* 34). *Hablar* and *chicas* then left Spain's keyword field, and *mujeres* and *entrenador* took their place.

Popularity isn't difficulty. Citas, rencontre and dating are owned by big dating apps, so Wingman will rank low there at first. The smaller relevant terms (ligar, hablar, mujeres, chicas, consejos, coach, drague, flirt) are the realistic wins. Recheck rankings 2–3 weeks after 1.16 is live.
