## Name (30)

**Recommended (Mexico):** `Wingman: Hablar con mujeres` (27)

Alternative: `Wingman: Coqueteo y confianza` (29)

The English name is "Wingman: Dating & Confidence". As in German, the name and subtitle carry the most search weight, so they should hold what people type.
- **"hablar con mujeres"** is the plainest description of what the app teaches, and it is how people phrase the problem (*cómo hablar con mujeres*). In a quick search (2026-11-15) the Spanish-language competition was thin: Labia ("Coach para hablar con mujeres", no rating shown), Alpha ("Asistente de Citas") and a paid guide, "Entender a las Mujeres". Most English coaching apps (Smooth, Approach, Vibing, Rizzma) show English listings in the Mexican store. So Wingman could rank for it.
- **"ligar"** is crowded by dating apps (Kismia: "App de citas y ligar"), so it sits in the subtitle, not the name. The same choice German made with *flirten*.
- **Another app called "Wingman"** (an AI dating coach) exists; a different second half keeps the two apart, as in German.

**Spanish (Spain):** `Wingman: Ligar con confianza` (28). In Spain *ligar* is the everyday word and less owned by dating apps; *hablar con mujeres* moves to the subtitle.

## Keywords (100)

**Mexico:**

```
conocer,coquetear,confianza,citas,timidez,autoestima,carisma,conversación,coach,mismo,hombres,ligue
```

99 characters.
- **Commas only, no spaces.** Words already in the name or subtitle (wingman, hablar, con, mujeres, aprende, ligar, seguridad) are left out, because Apple combines all three fields. *seguridad* (subtitle) + *mismo* (keywords) covers *seguridad en mí mismo*; *conocer* + *mujeres* covers *conocer mujeres*; *ligar* + *ligue* covers both forms.
- **Accents:** written with them (*conversación*). People often type without; whether the App Store matches both is worth checking in App Store Connect's search after launch.
- **Left out on purpose:**
  - *seducir*, *seducción*, *conquistar*, *pickup*: the pickup-artist tone the brief rules out.
  - *ansiedad*, *ansiedad social*: the results there are mental-health apps, and Wingman makes no health claims.
  - *chavas*, *morras*, *chicas guapas*: slang, regional, or the wrong tone.

**Spanish (Spain):**

```
conocer,coquetear,citas,timidez,autoestima,carisma,conversación,coach,mismo,hombres,ligue,seguridad
```

99 characters. *seguridad* moves here because the Spain subtitle drops it; *confianza* is in the Spain name.

## Screenshots: how to make the Spanish set

As for German:

1. **Copy the frames** in Figma and name them "ES 1" to "ES 5".
2. **Change the words** with the Spanish above. Spanish runs about 20–25 % longer than English, so expect to shrink a size or move a line break in the headlines (images 1 and 2 most of all).
3. **Redo the hand-lettering** ("Menos vueltas, más conversación"), set it in a handwriting font, or drop it from the Spanish set.
4. **Change the phone contents** from the tables above. Fresh Spanish captures on the simulator are possible, but **each capture run creates a guest user in production**, so they need Arthur's OK.
5. **Export at 1284 × 2778** and upload under the Spanish localization of the version.

If no Spanish screenshots are uploaded, the Spanish page shows the English ones.
