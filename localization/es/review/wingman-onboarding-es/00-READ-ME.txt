Wingman onboarding in Spanish: 22 screens
==========================================

Screens 01-17: onboarding, from the landing screen to the paywall.
Screens 18-22: the first-run walkthrough a new user sees after the paywall.

Captured on an iPhone 17 Pro simulator on 15 Nov 2026 (half resolution).


What the Spanish is meant to be
-------------------------------
One Spanish for every market: neutral Latin American Spanish that a
Spaniard also reads naturally. These choices are deliberate, so please
do not "correct" them to Spain's usage:

  - tu everywhere; plural "ustedes", never "vosotros"
  - "celular" (not "movil"), "Configuracion" (not "Ajustes")
  - curly quotation marks, not angle quotes

When something reads oddly, it helps to say which kind of problem it is:

  A. Wrong or unclear in any Spanish (grammar, typo, clumsy, machine-like)
  B. Fine, but a Spaniard would say it differently
  C. Sounds strange or off-putting to a Spaniard
